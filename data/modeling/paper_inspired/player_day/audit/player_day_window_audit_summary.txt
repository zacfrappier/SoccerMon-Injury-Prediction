================================================================================
SoccerMon Paper-Inspired Player-Day Window Audit
================================================================================

Window definition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
History window: 21 prior calendar days
Future follow-up: 7 calendar days
Prediction day itself is not included in either history or future outcome.

Candidate windows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All possible player-day windows: 11,112

Strict complete multimodal windows: 0
Full modality-range windows: 6,640
Partial multimodal windows: 6,431

7-day injury-positive windows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Strict multimodal positive windows: 0
Full modality-range positive windows: 7

Unique injury-event support
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observed injury-event days: 6
Injury events with >=1 strict multimodal prediction window: 0
Injury events with >=1 full-range prediction window: 1
Injury events with >=1 partial multimodal prediction window: 0

Eligibility definitions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Calendar eligible: full 21-day history, full 7-day future follow-up, and no injury event on prediction day.
Strict multimodal: all 21 history days contain subjective, training, and objective observations.
Full modality range: all 21 history days fall inside the observed date ranges of all three modalities, although individual values may still be missing.
Partial multimodal: at least one subjective, one training, and one objective observation occur somewhere within the 21-day history.

Important interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This audit does not select the final DeepHit eligibility rule.
It separates structural modality absence from within-range missingness so that later imputation decisions do not manufacture data outside actual observation periods.
Positive-window counts are not equivalent to unique injuries because one injury can appear in several prediction windows during the 7-day forecast horizon.
