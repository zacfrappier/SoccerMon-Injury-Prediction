Reconstruction assumptions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SoccerMon speed is interpreted as metres per second.
2. Repeated GPS/speed rows are collapsed to one observation per unique timestamp.
3. Timestamp intervals longer than 1.0 second are excluded from distance and zone-time integration.
4. Speed zones use reconstruction thresholds:
   low < 3.33 m/s
   medium 3.33-4.44 m/s
   high >4.44-5.55 m/s
   sprint >5.55 m/s
5. Heart rate is not used in the initial paper-inspired objective feature set.
6. Accelerometer and gyroscope signals are intentionally reserved for later improved-model experiments.
