Reconstruction assumptions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. srpe_total is used as Daily Load because the paper defines daily load as the sum of session RPE values across the day.
2. ATL is the rolling 7-day mean of Daily Load.
3. Weekly Load is the rolling 7-day sum of Daily Load.
4. Monotony is reconstructed as the rolling 7-day mean divided by rolling 7-day standard deviation.
5. Strain is reconstructed as Weekly Load multiplied by Monotony.
6. CTL28 and CTL42 are rolling sums over 28 and 42 calendar days, respectively.
7. ACWR is reconstructed as 7-day mean load divided by 28-day mean load because the manuscript does not specify the chronic denominator.
8. Missing training records remain missing rather than being automatically interpreted as zero-load days.
9. Past injury count uses only injuries occurring before the current player-day to prevent leakage.
10. No feature imputation is performed in this script.
